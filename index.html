How we work

Every room starts as a model.

  • 01

    Listen

    A first meeting on site or on a call, to understand the space, the budget and how you live in it.

  • 02

    Design

    A space plan, a material palette and 3D views, refined with you before anything is ordered.

  • 03

    Build

    Turnkey execution with our own site team, weekly site visits and a fixed schedule.

  • 04

    Handover

    Styling, a final walkthrough, and one year of support after you move in.
